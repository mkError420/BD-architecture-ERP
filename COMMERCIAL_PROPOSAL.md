## 1. Executive Summary

In today’s fast-paced construction and real estate landscape in Bangladesh, managing multiple project sites, fluctuating raw material costs (Rod, Cement, Sand, Bricks), daily labor muster rolls (Masons, Rod Binders, Helpers), and regulatory approvals (RAJUK, CDA, KDA, RDA) across distributed teams creates massive operational friction and cost overruns.

**Buildium-solution ERP** is a full-stack, customized enterprise management platform engineered specifically for the Bangladeshi construction and architecture sector. It bridges the gap between the head office and active project sites, providing real-time financial transparency, material inventory control, daily labor wage liability tracking, and automated billing compliant with National Board of Revenue (NBR) standards.

## 3. Scope of Software Modules

1. **📊 Executive Command Dashboard**
   - Real-time project KPI cards, Active budgets vs. Actual spend, Net cash flow analytics.
   - Project distribution charts and site progress gauges.
2. **🏢 Multi-Project Lifecycle Management**
   - Multi-district/division tracking across Bangladesh (Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, etc.).
   - Landowner details, BNBC specification compliance, and budget allocation.
3. **👷 Labor & Engineering Workforce Management**
   - Role-based worker database (রাজমিস্ত্রি, রডবাইন্ডার, হেলপার, Electrician, Site Engineer).
   - NID verification, blood group registry, and emergency contact details.
4. **✅ Daily Site Attendance & Wage Engine**
   - Batch site check-in/out, overtime calculation, automated daily wage liabilities.
5. **🧱 Localized Material & Inventory Catalog**
   - Bangla categorized construction items (সিমেন্ট, বালু, ইট, রড, খোয়া, রং, ইত্যাদি).
   - Unit conversion, warehouse stock management, and stock requisition.
6. **🚚 Supplier & Vendor Management**
   - Vendor profiles, trade license tracking, payment terms (COD, Credit, LC).
7. **📋 Site Work Orders & Milestone Tracking**
   - Step-by-step milestone execution (Foundation, Structure, Masonry, Finishing).
8. **💰 Financial Management, Invoicing & Payroll**
   - Categorized expense tracking (Material, Labor, Utility, Equipment).
   - Tax-compliant invoicing with line items, 15% VAT calculation, discount logic.
   - Monthly salary disbursement sheets with mobile banking export.
9. **📁 Compliance & Digital Document Archive**
   - Secure storage for municipal permits, CAD layouts, and laboratory reports.
10. **📈 Executive Audit Reports & Analytics**
    - Instant PDF/Print export for Project P&L, Material Consumption, Labor Wage summaries.
11. **👥 Multi-Role Security & Permissions**
    - Granular access control for Admin, Project Manager, Site Engineer, Accountant, and Site Supervisor.
